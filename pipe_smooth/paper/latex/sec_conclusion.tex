\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We formalized topology-preserving, multi-usage pipe centerline refinement under
permanent tubular occupancy, and instantiated it with hybrid trunk-share /
junction-split Hermite variables, soft path / clearance / union / spacing
costs, and hard clearance at accept.
On $50$ hard multi-group synthetic instances with capsule clearance, the refine
reaches full hard-accept success, $\kappa r_{\max}\lesssim 1$, and about a
$95\%$ relative drop in the soft objective.

The objective here is geometric: $K$ is a bend proxy, not pressure drop, and
capsule clearance is a fast narrowphase rather than exact CAD interference.
Fluid PDE polish and automatic weight schedules are left to later work; this
report only claims a usable geometric stage between discrete multi-group
routing and subsequent solid / physics optimization.
